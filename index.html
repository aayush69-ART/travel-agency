The Dayara Promise

We are not a city company. We are mountain people from Raithal & Uttarkashi. For us, these treks are home. When you travel with us, you walk like a guest of the mountains — slow, safe, and respectfully.

Local Expertise

Our guides grew up on these slopes. Expect shortcuts through oak forests, local stories by the fire, and winter tips that only villagers know.

Small Groups

No crowded trails, no shouting. We keep batches intimate so you still hear the crunch of snow and wind in the pines — not speakers and whistles.

Safety First

Every trek is backed with mountain–grade gear, route planning, oxygen, pulse oximeter and a trained local leader who knows when to push and when to turn back.
